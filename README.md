# Circular Battery Systems for Mongolia

## Vision
Building a sustainable battery lifecycle system for Mongolia.

## Executive Overview

Mongolia is entering an early but accelerating phase of electric vehicle (EV) adoption.
This project assesses the long-term implications of EV growth on battery waste volumes and on the recycling capacity Mongolia may need.

It is part of the Mongolia Circular Battery Initiative (MCBI): [mcbis.org](https://mcbis.org)

---

## Project Objective

To determine:

- When EV battery waste will emerge at scale
- What recycling capacity could be economically justified
- Whether immediate large-scale investment is viable
- What phased infrastructure pathway minimizes financial risk

## Preliminary Findings

These are early scenario estimates. The waste, capacity and financial models behind them are not yet published in this repository and will be validated in a feasibility study.

- Battery waste is likely to stay moderate until around 2033
- An immediate large (around 5,000 t/year) industrial plant does not look justified
- A smaller modular preprocessing hub (around 1,000–1,500 t/year) may become viable later
- Phased scaling reduces capital exposure and market risk

---

## Phased Pathway

**Phase 1 (2026–2029): Data and diagnostics foundation**
- Digital lab: battery-health models on open datasets (in progress)
- Pilot battery registration system: [passport.mcbis.org](https://passport.mcbis.org) (non-official pilot)
- Battery diagnostics lab pilot: collection, safe storage, testing and sorting only, with no dismantling, shredding or metal extraction. See the [proposal](proposals/battery-diagnostics-lab-pilot-en.md).

**Phase 2 (after feasibility study): Preprocessing**
- Modular preprocessing hub, only if the feasibility study and real battery volumes support it. See the [low-carbon recycling plant concept note](proposals/recycling-plant-concept.md).

**Phase 3 (post-2033): Scale-up**
- Larger capacity only if sustained waste exceeds around 3,000 t/year.

## Project Status

Research and pilot planning stage. No laboratory or plant has been built yet.
Open to discussion with partners, funders and policy makers.

Mongolia does not yet have a battery EPR law. The EPR materials in this repository are independent MCBI proposals, not official government drafts.
